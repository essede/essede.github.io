Latest AD (September 2026 Campaign)

Vote ESSEDE to the House of Representatives this September! Let’s cut away all the fluff. Here’s what I stand for:


-- No more overreach The federal government has too much power, and it’s overreaching hard. I plan to fight for the rights of citizens to make their own choices without regulation, and to return the autonomy of City Councils - the bodies closer to the people, and elected directly by their local constituents. The Town Rights Expansion Act is too weak - it grants towns their basic rights, but keeps the federal government too powerful.


-- End the unneeded bureaucracy Let’s face it - the federal government can be slow. It shouldn’t be entrusted to micromanage everything, and it’s filled with departments that do way too much, and are way too centralised. I will work to ensure that towns get more autonomy over local healthcare, education, and local commerce, so that the federal government can’t get bogged down with tasks that should be local.

-- People first Leave the local stuff to the local governments. I’ve made it clear that I will fight to make that happen, and that the federal government does too much. But why? I believe that it is the right of every person to live without interference from a distant government that does everything. And it is also their right to have control over their local affairs - and that’s why I will push for the decentralisation of government - not to make the federal government powerless, but to give towns more autonomy.


If I am elected to the house, I will work to make this happen. And if I'm not, I will respect the choice of the voters - it’s the core principle of my views that governance choices should come down to the people.
